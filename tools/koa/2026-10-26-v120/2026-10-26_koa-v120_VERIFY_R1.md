## 6. Summary

Approximately 230 discrete claims were extracted and checked: 93 arithmetic or sum checks in `arith.py`, 48 supplement pointer-existence checks, 66 reference entries and all in-text citations in both directions, and about 120 text-to-table or text-to-supplement value and direction comparisons. Of these, about 200 verified, **7 are contradicted** (C1 the abstract's 85% vs 58% everywhere else, C2 the reversed order of the ln(BAL+1) coefficients, C3 the Woollons 2008/1998 year mismatch, C4 the planted-support lower bound 158 vs 109, C5 Table 6's DOFAW source ratio 0.88 absent from Table S12, C6 "QMD does not fall with density" vs Table S31, C7 natural thinning QMD gain 3.4 vs 3.3) and **19 groups of claims are unverified** because the package holds no table or section carrying the value (U1 to U19). Counts: abstract 300 words, main text 9,868 words (9,727 excluding heading lines), 8 keywords, 6 tables, 6 figures, 66 references, supplement 33 tables and 8 figures; all agree with the cover letter.
