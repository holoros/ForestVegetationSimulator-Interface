# Changelog

## 1.15.0 (November 21, 2026)

Changes are from version 1.14.3. The 53 Kapapala (Kap) plots carried one fill value of BYI; they now carry BYI predicted from their own climate and soils by the statewide forest model (scripts 65 and 66). Maximum SDI, the joint height curve and height increment, diameter increment and mortality were refit on the new BYI with forms unchanged; crown base (E3e) and ingrowth are unchanged. Maximum SDI is S1 = exp(4.0231 + 0.5408 ln BYI - 0.461 planted), or 1,340 trees per ha (95% interval 1,194 to 1,488) natural and 541 (448 to 620) planted at each origin's median BYI. The pre-registered height rule of script 45 (narrowest asymptote interval) now picks the anchored form A2 (interval ratio 1.097 against 1.106 for the joint form J1); J1 is kept for its lower held-out error and single height system, and the choice is reported. Held out by installation, height increment passes the mean bias test but its slope (0.85, 90% interval 0.73 to 0.99) falls below the region. Script 67 builds `koa_rdbyi_equations.csv`; `koa_project` reproduces script 51 to 8.33e-15.

The planted scenario list is now chosen by rule: the planted visit with at least 30 trees closest to the median QMD and stems of such visits (a planted plot as measured in 2015, 1,834 stems per ha, QMD 7.8 cm). The earlier 11-tree list produced the spacing failure of the first Bakuzis run. Equations are unchanged by this choice. Script 68 runs the full modified Bakuzis matrix (two origins, BYI at the 5th to 95th percentiles, four starting densities, 200 years): site ordering passes everywhere, mortality rises with density, self-thinning slopes run from -1.64 to -2.19 (natural at 4,000 stems per ha -2.27 to -2.30), basal area and SDI stay below the observed 99.9th percentiles, planted QMD falls with starting density to 40 years, and Eichhorn's rule fails (CV 0.13 to 0.24). Script 69 tests the three stand-density terms of PREREG section 10 (written before fitting); none was eligible. Scripts 70 and 71 draw Fig. 6 and Figs. S1 to S3 and S5.

Benchmark (53 plots, 182 plot-horizons): RMSE 0.126 survival, 2.65 cm QMD, 6.76 m2 per ha basal area; 90% band coverage 89%, 87% and 70%. A single SDImax of 742 does as well (basal area RMSE difference +0.154, 95% interval -0.066 to 0.300; script 25). HiGy 0.5.1 carries these equations. Script 72 rebuilds the workbook grid for the Forest Solutions financial model from the rule-picked planted list (the earlier grid used the 11-tree list), and build_workbook_1150.py fills the workbook (out/workbook_1150). Scripts 33, 33b, 42 (height increment rounds one to three) and 35 (stand-level mortality forms) were rerun under 1.15.0 for Tables S33 and S35; no stand-level form is preferred, and in the round-three rerun B5, B7 and B8 pass the adoption rule but B2 stays. In an FVS-HI keyfile the keywords of HiByi.kcp must sit inside each stand (FVS-Online inserts an attached kcp this way); an AddFile record pointing to the kcp did not load, and the stands then ran without growth. Manuscript v141 and supplement v133 carry 1.15.0. Published on Zenodo as 10.5281/zenodo.24425094 (November 21, 2026).

## 1.14.3 (November 21, 2026)

Changes are from version 1.14.2. The crown record used for every crown base fit since 2025 stored HCB = HT x CR for all 360 US Forest Service FIA koa trees, where CR is the FIA live (compacted) crown ratio, so the response was live crown length and not crown base. CR behaves as a crown ratio (it falls as koa BAL rises, Spearman -0.43, and rises with relative height, 0.26). Crown base is HT x (1 - CR), with a median crown ratio of 0.25 (5th to 95th percentile 0.05 to 0.60). On the corrected response every competition term takes the expected sign without constraint. Script 63 compares 13 forms the system can apply (koa-only competition, BYI, no climate) on the folds of record, and the rule of record (fewest coefficients within 2% of the lowest held-out RMSE among sign-consistent forms) picks E3e: HCB = HT / (1 + exp(b0 + lsl ln(HT/DBH) + sbalba_k sqrt(BAL BA + 1) + lba_k ln(BA + 1))), b0 = -0.829 (95% interval -1.163 to -0.427), lsl = -0.444 (-0.760 to -0.134), sbalba_k = -0.0397 (-0.0816 to 0), lba_k = -0.099 (-0.364 to 0), plot-cluster bootstrap with 2,000 resamples (the last two reach the sign bound in 9% and 23% of resamples). Held out, E3e has a bias of -0.01 m, an RMSE of 2.20 m, R2 0.74 and a slope of 1.07 (90% interval 0.97 to 1.16), and passes both equivalence tests; E3d refitted to the corrected response scores 2.69 m. Scripts 59 to 62 fitted crown length and are superseded. Nothing else in the system uses crown base, so basal area, stems, QMD, SDI, RD, height, the benchmark and the gates are unchanged. Script 64 (Table S36 of supplement v132) reports crown ratio in the 200-year runs under E3e: stand values 0.15 to 0.30, lower in the smallest than the largest fifth of stems, inside the observed range. Script 64b redraws Fig. 3c and Fig. S4c. `koa_step_rdbyi.R`: `koa_hcb(dbh, ht, bal, ba, cf)`. HiGy carries E3e from commit 4e3c391. Manuscript v140 and supplement v132 carry 1.14.3. Published on Zenodo as 10.5281/zenodo.24418330 (November 21, 2026).

## 1.14.2 (November 20, 2026)

Changes are from version 1.14.1. Crown base E3c, HCB = HT / (1 + exp(b0 + lH ln HT + x (BAL/BA) RD)), fitted a longer crown with more competition. Two sets of alternatives were tested on the same 360 US Forest Service FIA koa trees (Cardinal jobs 16999243 and 17000073): 17 crown ratio forms (script 59) and 15 forms fitted directly to crown base as a height (script 60), with koa-only and all-species basal area, BAL, relative BAL, relative height, DBH/HT, BYI, rainfall and elevation. Every competition and density term fitted the unexpected way, and with the expected sign it sat at zero. E3d replaces E3c: HCB = min(exp(b0 + lH ln HT), 0.98 HT), b0 = -1.4985 (95% interval -2.900 to -0.306) and lH = 1.0781 (0.648 to 1.551), plot-cluster bootstrap with 2,000 resamples. On the folds of record (script 61, which reproduces the logged held-out RMSE of C0, C1 and C2), E3d has a bias of -0.30 m, an RMSE of 2.70 m against 2.26 m for E3c, and a slope of 0.56 (90% interval 0.32 to 0.86), and fails both equivalence tests (smallest passing region 25.4% for the mean). The one-parameter form HCB = 0.278 HT scored 2.60 m; E3d was kept by the authors' choice. The change is post hoc and disclosed. Nothing else in the system uses crown base, so basal area, stems, QMD, SDI, RD, height, the benchmark and the gates are unchanged. The fitting frame (rebuilt from HI_TREE.csv; md5 093c759addd9e713ee42b98fd6524266) carries plot identifiers and is not deposited.

Script 62 is script 53 with E3d (Table S36 of supplement v131; `out/longterm_metrics_62.csv`): crown ratio differs by less than 0.01 between the smallest and largest fifth of stems. Script 62b redraws Fig. S4 with panel (c) from E3d held out on the folds of record (RMSE 2.696 m, as script 61). It builds the folds under R's default generator, as script 34 did; `32_common.R` sets L'Ecuyer-CMRG, under which the folds differ.

## 1.13.0 (November 11, 2026)

Status: published November 11, 2026, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24116922. Changes are from version 1.12.2 (November 7, 2026).

### Why the earlier engine was replaced

An audit of the deposited v105final engine on November 10, 2026 found that the model it runs differs from the model its documentation describes. On the deployed path the audit counted 85 numeric constants and 4 rules. Of the 85, 38 are fitted, 4 come from the literature, 9 are calibrated and 34 are hand set, so 47 of the 85 are not fitted; 13 of those 47 were shown or judged material. Mortality Stage 1, the Stage 3 tree equation and the natural-stand mortality level factor are defined in the code and never applied. One mortality function is never called anywhere in the folder. The mortality step that runs is a self-thinning step with hand-set floors, and diameter and height increment are scaled by solved origin multipliers.

### What was done, in order

A first refit on November 10, 2026 applied four fitted equations with no multiplier, floor or cap. It failed in the long run: on the real starting lists it failed the basal area bound, the level gate and the Sukachev relation.

A relative-density framework followed the same day. Competition in the diameter increment equation became (BAL / BA) × RD, and mortality gained a DBH² term and ln(RD), where RD is the stand density index of live koa over a maximum (SDImax). It was applied to a new stand with a single SDImax of 742.

On November 11, 2026 SDImax was modeled from BYI and origin (S1), and the increment and mortality equations were refitted with the SDImax they are applied with. The rule written before the test did not prefer this form. The author adopted S1 for consistency between fitting and use.

Later that day a defect was found in the November 10 frames. The plantation permanent sample plot (PSP) database records thinned stems as dead, and the frames did not censor removal intervals, so every fit and benchmark until then counted thinning removals as deaths: 756 of 7,478 mortality tree-intervals and 324 of 1,169 deaths. Intervals that end in or span a removal year are now censored (6,722 tree-intervals and 845 deaths remain), and benchmark horizons stop before the first removal year (53 plots and 182 plot-horizons remain). S1, the height equation and the diameter increment equation are unchanged. Mortality, benchmark, gates, projections and coverage changed. Under the correction the rule still does not prefer S1 (benchmark basal area RMSE 6.74 m² ha⁻¹ against 6.60 for 742), and the Sukachev gate fails.
