## Verdict
- **dDBH: K3p passes the preregistered rule and would replace K0.** K3p = tree's BAL share x RD in place of BAL (both BAL terms), RD in place of BAPH,
  RD = SDI / 923 for every plot. Signs pass; ΔAIC -103.74; better than K0 on 3 of 4 held-out sources (FIA 1.7630 vs 2.4145, KMR PSP 0.6053 vs 0.6108,
  PSP 0.9823 vs 0.9857; worse on DOFAW 1.0635 vs 1.0529); C2 site coefficient b8 0.2595, bootstrap 95% 0.0664 to 0.4989 (200 of 200), excludes zero.
  It is a population-SDImax variant, so it can be projected. No other candidate passes, so there is no tie.
- **dHT: no candidate passes; K0 stays** and the dHT results are a sensitivity. Every candidate is worse than K0 in AIC (ΔAIC +16.8 to +118.7) and better on only
  1 of 4 held-out sources.
- **Does RD weighting help?** For diameter growth, only in the relative form (K3p) and only with the population SDImax: in-sample fit improves strongly, but the
  held-out gain comes mostly from FIA (RMSE 2.41 -> 1.76); the other three sources change by 0.004 to 0.011, one of them (DOFAW) worse. With plot-specific SDImax the candidates are better than K0 on fewer
  held-out sources than their population variants (K1 2 vs K1p 3, K2 0 vs K2p 1, K3 2 vs K3p 3) (AIC: K1 lower than K1p by 17.3, K2 and K3 higher than K2p and K3p by 0.9 and 56.7), consistent with the shrunk,
  single-row plot SDImax values (brms job: 44.6% of plots single-row). Multiplying BAL and BAPH by RD (K2, K2p) flips the BAPH sign for dDBH. RD in place of BAPH
  alone (K1p) is nearly collinear with BAPH (cor 0.966) and gives ΔAIC -2.06 only. For height growth RD does not help in any form.

## Caveats on the adopted dDBH candidate K3p (reported; none is part of the rule)
1. The b6 (RD) coefficient is negative (-0.0795, model SE 0.0254) but its bootstrap interval spans zero (-0.2512 to 0.1520); b5 (ln CR) also spans zero
   (-0.0640 to 1.7021). The BAL-share x RD terms carry the competition effect (b4 -3.7228, -4.8590 to -3.1678).
2. b3 (x^2 / ln(DBH+5)) is positive (2.2116; 0.9701 to 4.8899), so the BAL-type effect stops decreasing at large x = share x RD: 0.26% of dDBH rows lie beyond
   the turning point (out/diag_bal_turning.csv; x median 0.130, 95th pct 0.629, max 3.245). From the K3p point estimates the turning point is x of 0.83 at
   DBH 1 cm, 0.98 at 5 cm and 1.22 at 20 cm (solving 2 b3 x (1+x) = -b4 ln(DBH+5)); suppressed trees in stands with RD near or above 1 would sit there in projection.
3. Strata: the 40-60 cm ratio is unchanged (1.7007 vs 1.7013); the BAL Q5 ratio worsens (1.2253 vs 1.1628); overall ratio 1.0504 vs 1.0434.
4. Site response: increment ratio high vs low level exp(b8 ln(418/142)) 1.323 (K3p) vs 1.350 (K0). Origin constants c natural 0.3494, planted 1.1211
   (K0 0.4163, 1.2596).
5. K0 was not bootstrapped with the same draws (stopped, RUN.md); the deployed v105 K0 b8 interval is 0.0523 to 0.5322 (B 400, another seed;
   ~/jobs/koa_v105_20261102/RESULTS_v105.md).
6. Plot SDImax with RD in K3 (plot-specific) did not hold up out of sample (2 of 4 sources), so the gain depends on using the single population value.

## Step 3: what would change in the simulator if K3p is deployed for dDBH (not done; the engine was not modified)
- Each annual step, per tree record (or cohort): BAL share = BAL / BAPH, which is the fraction stand_bal() already forms before multiplying by BAPH
  (koa_equations.py: bal_percentile_fraction(_weighted) on the exact path, bal_cohort_linear_fraction on the cohort path); TPH = sum of live expansion factors,
  QMD = sqrt(BAPH / TPH / 0.00007854), SDI = TPH (QMD/25.4)^1.605 from the same live list that gives BAPH (all species), RD = SDI / 923 (one value for every stand;
  no origin-specific SDImax was tested). x = share x RD.
- DDBH: b3 x^2 / ln(DBH+5) + b4 ln(x+1) replaces b3 BAL^2/ln(DBH+5) + b4 ln(BAL+1), and b6 sqrt(RD x DBH) replaces b6 sqrt(BAPH x DBH); the K3p vector
  (out/coefficients.csv) replaces dDBH_L_NO in koa_equations/koa_params and HiGy.R. Stand terms move with the current year's state as they do now (the fit
  interpolates t.0 to t.1 linearly).
- Unchanged: HCB/CR (still BALl and BAPH), dHT (K0), mortality, ingrowth.
- Must be re-derived before use: CF_DDBH_MARGINAL, CAL_DDBH and CAL_SE_LOG (the 400-draw boot_v103 machinery on the K3p form), then the chains that read the dDBH
  vector (Table 4, culmination, plot-level and trajectory validation, LOIO, paired MC, Bakuzis, containment), with an explicit check of item 2 above in dense
  projected stands.
